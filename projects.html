Policy & Intervention on Regulation

Arsenic Demonstration Sites

A retrospective cost and performance analysis of EPA's 50 full-scale arsenic treatment pilot sites, examining outcomes following the 2001 arsenic MCL reduction from 50 µg/L to 10 µg/L. This work supports the interactive Shiny dashboard on the Publications page and has been presented at AWWA WQTC, SWFWRC/AWRA, and FSAWWA conferences.

Distribution System Nitrification Action Plans

A multi-level intervention analysis with the Association of State Drinking Water Administrators (ASDWA) evaluating statewide nitrification action plans and their impact on distribution-system nitrification, disinfectant residuals, and disinfection byproducts (MDBPs). Published as "Evaluating state nitrification action plans for controlling distribution system nitrification and MDBPs" in AWWA Water Science (read here). Presented at AWWA WQTC, FSAWWA, FWRC, and SWFWRC/AWRA conferences.

Biofiltration Research

Biofilm Management & Modeling

Research on biofilter design and management for micropollutant removal, including deep-learning-based prediction models (LSTM, Neural ODE, SINDy) and microbial community dynamics, supporting Natchaya's Ph.D. dissertation on biofilm microbiome and emerging contaminant degradation.

Open PDF in new tab →

Complex Systems Modeling

A comparison of traditional kinetic models and system dynamics representations of substrate removal in biofilters, examining how complex systems modeling approaches capture microbial community behavior and treatment performance — part of the broader biofilter modeling work supporting Natchaya's Ph.D. dissertation.

Open PDF in new tab →

Biofilm Detachment Method for Microbial Analysis

Co-developed a novel biofilm-recovery method with the Southern Nevada Water Authority (SNWA) that improves the extraction of microbial material from granular activated carbon filters for molecular and flow-cytometric analysis. Published as "Enhancing biofilm recovery from carbon filters for molecular analysis and flow cytometry" in AWWA Water Science (read here).

RHI & PFAS Research

Relative Health Indicator

Applied and extended the Relative Health Indicator (RHI) metric to quantify potential health-risk reductions from PFAS regulation under varying standards, identifying significant regulatory opportunities. This work supported "The impact of the fourth regulatory determination on vulnerable populations," published in Environmental Monitoring and Assessment (read here).
