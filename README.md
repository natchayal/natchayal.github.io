# Jade's Field Notes

**Natchaya "Jade" Luangphairin** · Ph.D. Candidate, Civil & Environmental Engineering, University of South Florida

Welcome to the repository behind my personal portfolio. It's a space for drinking water research, data, policy, outreach, and everything I'm learning along the way.

My work sits where drinking water treatment, data analytics, and policy meet. I turn complex research and operational data into clear, practical decisions for utilities, public agencies, and communities. My research covers PFAS and other emerging contaminants, biofiltration, potable reuse, EPA regulatory-data analysis, and the use of microbial community dynamics and agent-based modeling to understand biofilm development.

- **About & Experience**: my professional summary, academic journey, and CV
- **Projects & Dashboards**: research projects and interactive data tools
- **Publications**: peer-reviewed articles and project write-ups
- **Teaching**: courses, mentorship, and workshops
- **Skills & Awards**, **Gallery**, and **Contact**
